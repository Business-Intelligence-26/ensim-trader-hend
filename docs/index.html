Replay assumptions
Each strategy starts with 1,000 cash and 10 units of each product. Orders use information through the prior tick and fill at the next recorded price. The replay applies a 50 cash reserve, forbids short selling, and excludes fees and market impact. The random baselines use seed 42 and the same 10-unit cap.

      How the course projects shaped this dashboard
    
        
      
      • TD1 · EU Capitals starts from a decision and brings source data into a published dashboard. TP2 applies that pattern to observed prices and trade decisions. TD1 repository

      • TD2 · Smart Mobility documents proxy assumptions. TP2 labels the starting portfolio, execution rules, and benchmark seed as replay assumptions. TD2 repository

      • TD3 · Scraping to KPI turns source records into defined measures. TP2 converts price ticks into three-tick averages and portfolio values into return and drawdown. TD3 repository
        • 
      
      • TD4 · Time Series motivates the trailing average. TP2 compares two consecutive averages to produce a signal, and evaluates that signal on the next tick. TD4 repository
        • 
      
      • TD5 · Dashboard Design chooses charts around specific actions. Price and average lines support signal review, indexed lines support strategy comparison, and the drawdown chart shows how long losses persisted. TD5 repository
        • 
      
      • TP1 · Smart Internship Match contributes the overview-to-detail layout. KPI cards summarize the session, while the product filter and decision log let you inspect the evidence. TP1 repository
